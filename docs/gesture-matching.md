# Gesture matching and capture

Matching defaults to Auto. With no detected hands, only face profiles compete. With hands visible, only trained gestures with the same hand count compete. Face only always excludes gesture profiles. Failed/stale hand tracking pauses Auto; users can select Face only.

Hand descriptors come from the existing 21 landmarks. Coordinates account for camera aspect ratio so portrait and landscape inputs use consistent geometry. Each hand has 19 shape values (joint angles, finger reach, spread/pinch), 6 palm-axis direction values and 8 face-relative position/proximity values. Comparisons try both hand orders and mirrored poses. The largest per-hand RMS error must pass each gate: shape ≤ 0.18, individual finger error ≤ 0.4, direction ≤ 0.3, position ≤ 0.12, individual position error ≤ 0.22. Near-mouth versus near-nose pointing also has to agree. After eligibility, the face and gesture distances contribute 40% and 60% of squared overall distance respectively. These are geometric similarity thresholds, not learned classification confidence. Clear the current selection as soon as it becomes ineligible; eligible challengers retain the 600 ms hold and 0.005 margin.

Existing proximity-only profiles cannot recover finger shape. Their face values are retained and their gestures require a new capture. No Way and Ancient Aliens require two hands; Thinking Monkey, Call Me Cat, Actually Cat, Nose Picking, Captain, Drake yes, Roll Safe and Salt Bae require one. Other remaining images are face-only; Agnes's background hands are incidental.

Capture allows Save before hands are visible. After a three-second preparation countdown, it requires at least 750 ms and six distinct fresh face/hand observations. Invalid or moving poses restart the collection window; a 15-second collection timeout leaves the prior profile unchanged. Face RMS drift is limited to 0.08; gesture drift limits are shape 0.1, direction 0.15 and position 0.08. Suspended tabs and repeated stale observations cannot complete capture. The median-time hand observation is saved to avoid averaging swapped left/right hands. Camera stop, dialog close, selection change and Cancel cancel collection.

Version 2 backups preserve pose requirements, validated gesture descriptors, overlay preference and matching mode. Version 1 imports retain facial/proximity values but require hand recapture. Known retired IDs are skipped before the shared validator; unrelated unknown IDs remain errors. Neutral calibration stays local and is excluded from exported/shared preferences. Existing local settings override src/data/sitePreferences.json.

Saved facial defaults live in src/data/profileDefaults.json so running memes:sync cannot overwrite training. Retired imported IDs are also removed from scripts/meme-defaults.json. Normal builds do no upstream fetching or analysis. The pre-change browser snapshot is retained locally in .cache/browser-before-gesture-update.json (ignored by Git).

The development watcher polls timestamps instead of cloud-backed filesystem events, avoiding false reloads during camera sessions. Bulk cache, test results and design artifacts are excluded from watching.
